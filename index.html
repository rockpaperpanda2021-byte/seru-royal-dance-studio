A Note From the Founder

Hello

I'm Mahathi Vijayaprakash, and VP Culture Club began as something I simply couldn't stop thinking about. Rooted in the spirit of our folk traditions — dance, storytelling, music, and the quiet joy of community gatherings — I wanted to build a place where culture isn't only something you watch from a seat, but something you live, share, and carry home with you.

For months now, I've been meeting a certain kind of person in my workshops. They usually smile, a little shy, and say something like —

And I understand it completely. Exams. Jobs. Children. Moving cities. Caring for parents. Somewhere along the way, the part of us that loved to perform, to listen, to create… quietly took a backseat. At VP Culture Club, I want to give that part of you a gentle way back.

Our sessions here in Bangalore are made especially for adults — for those who used to dance, sing, act, or tell stories when they were younger; who feel a quiet pull towards culture but aren't sure where to begin again; who worry they've become "rusty," "too old," or "out of touch." If any of that sounds like you, then you are exactly who I had in mind.
